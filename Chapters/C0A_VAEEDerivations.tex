\section{Optimal Gating Mechanism} \label{app:vaee:gating}

The gate of \cref{eq:methods:proba:alpha} ties a concept's
activation probability to the similarity between its predicted embedding and its
prototype. This section shows that this is not an arbitrary heuristic but the
mathematically optimal parametric form dictated by the Gated Gaussian prior of
\cref{eq:methods:proba:prior-z}, recovered by inverting the
generative flow with Bayes' rule.

Because the mask entry $c_k$ is binary, the posterior
$\nbmath{p(c_k = 1 \mid \vb{z}_k)}$ is fully characterised by its log-odds: the
sigmoid is the inverse of the logit transform\footnote{The logit transform is
defined as $\on{logit}(u) = \log \frac{u}{1 - u}$.}, so
\begin{equation*}
    \begin{split}
        p(c_k = 1 \mid \vb{z}_k)
        &= \on{sigmoid}\left( \log \frac{p(c_k = 1 \mid \vb{z}_k)}{1 - p(c_k = 1 \mid \vb{z}_k)} \right) \\
        &= \on{sigmoid}\left( \log \frac{p(c_k = 1 \mid \vb{z}_k)}{p(c_k = 0 \mid \vb{z}_k)} \right).
    \end{split}
\end{equation*}
Computing the posterior thus reduces to computing its log-odds, which by Bayes'
rule expand into a tractable function of the priors and likelihoods,
\begin{equation*}
    \log \frac{p(c_k = 1 \mid \vb{z}_k)}{p(c_k = 0 \mid \vb{z}_k)} =
    \underbrace{\log \frac{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 1)}{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 0)}}_{\text{likelihood ratio}} +
    \underbrace{\log \frac{p(c_k = 1)}{p(c_k = 0)}}_{=\, \on{logit}(\pi)}.
\end{equation*}
The prior ratio is just the logit of the Bernoulli activation rate $\pi$ of
\cref{eq:methods:proba:prior-c}, since
$\on{logit}(\pi) = \log\left(\flatfrac{\pi}{(1-\pi)}\right)$. For the likelihood
ratio we substitute the two branches of the Gated Gaussian prior,
$\nbmath{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 1)} = \mathcal{N}\left(\vb{z}_k;\, \vb{h}_k,\, \sigma_0^2 \vb{I}\right)$
and $\nbmath{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 0)} = \mathcal{N}\left(\vb{z}_k;\, \vb{0},\, \sigma_0^2 \vb{I}\right)$.
The two share the same isotropic covariance, so their $(2\pi\sigma_0^2)^{-d/2}$
normalising constants cancel inside the log-ratio, leaving only the
exponents,\footnote{Throughout the appendix, the notation $\norm{\,\cdot\,}$ is
used instead of $\norm{\,\cdot\,}_{2}$ to avoid making the notation excessively
verbose.}
\begin{equation*}
    \begin{split}
        \log \frac{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 1)}{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 0)}
        &= \log \frac{\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2\sigma_0^2}\norm{\vb{z}_k - \vb{h}_k}^2\right)}{\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2\sigma_0^2}\norm{\vb{z}_k}^2\right)} \\
        &= \frac{1}{2\sigma_0^2}\left(\norm{\vb{z}_k}^2 - \norm{\vb{z}_k - \vb{h}_k}^2\right).
    \end{split}
\end{equation*}
Expanding the squared distance,
$\norm{\vb{z}_k - \vb{h}_k}^2 = \norm{\vb{z}_k}^2 - 2\,\vb{z}_k \cdot \vb{h}_k + \norm{\vb{h}_k}^2$,
makes the $\norm{\vb{z}_k}^2$ terms cancel exactly, so the likelihood ratio
collapses to an inner product against the prototype,
\begin{equation*}
    \log \frac{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 1)}{p(\vb{z}_k \mid c_k = 0)} =
    \frac{1}{\sigma_0^2}\left(\vb{z}_k \cdot \vb{h}_k
    - \tfrac{1}{2}\norm{\vb{h}_k}^2\right).
\end{equation*}
Adding the prior log-odds back in, converting to a probability through the
sigmoid, and substituting the encoder's predicted mean $\vb*{\mu}_k(\vb{x})$ for
the embedding $\vb{z}_k$ yields an inner-product gate that is exact under the
Gated Gaussian prior,
\begin{equation} \label{eq:app:vaee:alpha-exact}
    \alpha_k(\vb{x}) = \on{sigmoid}\left(
    \frac{1}{\sigma_0^2}\left(\vb*{\mu}_k(\vb{x}) \cdot \vb{h}_k
    - \tfrac{1}{2}\norm{\vb{h}_k}^2\right) + \on{logit}(\pi)
    \right).
\end{equation}

To recover the form used in practice
(\cref{eq:methods:proba:alpha}), the additive constants
$-\frac{1}{2\sigma_0^2}\norm{\vb{h}_k}^2 + \on{logit}(\pi)$ are dropped and the
remaining scale $1/\sigma_0^2$ is folded into a single learnable inverse
temperature $1/\tau$, so that the logit is parameterised as
$(1/\tau)\,\vb*{\mu}_k(\vb{x}) \cdot \vb{h}_k$; the per-prototype bias is left
to be absorbed implicitly into $\tau$ and into the optimisation of $\vb{h}_k$
itself. Under the exact gate of \cref{eq:app:vaee:alpha-exact} the
decision boundary $\alpha_k = \tfrac{1}{2}$ sits where
$\vb*{\mu}_k \cdot \vb{h}_k = \tfrac{1}{2}\norm{\vb{h}_k}^2$,
which is well calibrated only as long as
$\norm{\vb*{\mu}_k} \approx \norm{\vb{h}_k}$; otherwise the model can inflate
$\norm{\vb*{\mu}_k}$ to push the inner product up and force a concept active.
The cosine-similarity variant of \cref{eq:methods:proba:sim} removes
this magnitude coupling by construction, which is why it is the choice of
similarity used in this thesis. Either way, the derivation shows that gating a
concept on the proximity of its embedding to its prototype is the optimal form
the Gated Gaussian prior prescribes.
